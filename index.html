Course Overview

View courses to help level up your kids math, science, and literacy skills.

Section 1.1

Introduction to Philosophy, Religion, and Other Dilemmas

Teaches kids about decision making, different traditions, and moral dilemmas such as free will, the trolley problem, and The Heinz Dilemma.

Section 1.2

Climate Change and Coding

Explore the different problems of climate change and discover coding websites for kids like Scratch, Tynker, and Code.org.

Section 1.3

Careers and Goals

Help kids research different careers and set goals for their future self in order to live a successful life.

Section 2.1

Algebra and Marine Biology

Teach children about the wonders of the sea and the awes of algebra.

Section 2.2

Reading "Of Mice and Men"

In this section, we read the classic "Of Mice and Men" in order to improve kids' literacy context.

Section 2.3

Anti-bullying PSA

In here, we teach kids how to stop bullying and the different types of bullying.

Section 2.4

Mental Health and Influences

This section teaches kids the importance of mental health and how social media influences them.

Section 2.5

Natural Disaster Survival and Wants vs. Needs

Teach kids about how to survive natural disasters and the difference between wants and needs.
